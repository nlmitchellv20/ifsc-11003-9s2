A Legacy of Exploration

Since 1997, NASA has successfully landed five robotic rovers on Mars, each building on the discoveries and engineering triumphs of the last:

Sojourner (1997):
The first wheeled vehicle to rove another planet, proving that low-cost, mobile surface exploration was possible.
Spirit & Opportunity (2004):
Twin robotic geologists that shattered expectations, uncovering definitive evidence that liquid water once flowed across the Martian surface.
Curiosity (2012 - Present):
A nuclear-powered mobile chemistry lab exploring Gale Crater, confirming that ancient Mars possessed the chemical building blocks and environmental conditions necessary to support microbial life.
Perseverance (2021 - Present):
NASA's most advanced astrobiology rover, exploring an ancient river delta in Jezero Crater, searching for biosignatures, and sealing rock cores for a future return to Earth.

The Mission

Every rover mission is driven by four core scientific and engineering goals:

  1. Determine if Life Ever Arose on Mars
  2. Characterize the Climateof Mars
  3. Characterize the Geology of Mars
  4. Prepare for Human Exploration

From rugged craters to ancient riverbeds, NASA's Mars rovers continue to push the boundaries of space exploration, bringing us closer to answering one of humanity's oldest questions: Are we alone in the universe?

The Five NASA Mars Rovers
Rover Mission Landed Status Key Focus & Achievements
Sojourner Mars Pathfinder July 1997 Completed (Sept. 1997) First wheeled vehicle to rove another planet; proved low-cost mobile exploration and analyzed Martian rocks.
Spirit Mars Exploration Rover Jan. 2004 Completed (March 2010) Explored Gusev Crater for six years and uncovered evidence of ancient hydrothermal activity and past water.
Opportunity Mars Exploration Rover Jan. 2004 Completed (June 2018) Operated for over 14 years at Meridiani Planum, discovering clear geological evidence of long-standing liquid water.
Curiosity Mars Science Laboratory Aug. 2012 Active Explored Gale Crater; confirmed ancient Mars had habitable environments, organic molecules, and persistent lakes.
Perseverance Mars 2020 Feb. 2021 Active Exploring Jezero Crater; deployed Ingenuity helicopter, caches rock samples; found potential biosignatures in "Cheyava Falls" rock.
