Sojourner

Sojourner was NASA's first successful Mars rover and the first wheeled vehicle to operate on another planet. The name Sojourner came from the African American abolitionist and women's rights activist Sojourner Truth.

Quick Facts

  • Launched: December 4, 1996
  • Landed: July 4, 1997
  • Mission Duration: 83 days
  • Distance Traveled: 100 meters

What is NASA?

NASA, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, is the United States government agency responsible for the nation's civilian space program and for aeronautics and aerospace research.
